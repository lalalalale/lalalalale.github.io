One road for each device

PCI Express connects the CPU to the chips and cards around it: graphics cards, SSDs, network cards. The old PCI bus made every card share one set of wires; PCIe gives each device a link of its own, point to point. Data travels on it in packets, the coloured trains here, slowed down about 25 million times.
