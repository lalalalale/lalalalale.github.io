Bits arrive, but no clock

Data reaches the receiver as a stream of high and low levels: here tall blocks are 1s and short blocks are 0s. No clock signal comes with it, so the receiver has to decide on its own when to look at each bit. Its reader looks once per bit, and the safest moment is the middle of a block, far from where the level changes.
